Easy Service for
Bangladeshi Citizens

A bilingual AI assistant designed to help citizens understand government paperwork, prepare documents, reduce errors, and navigate public services more confidently.

Read Project Proposal (PDF) Download Proposal

The Problem

Government-service information can be scattered, forms can be difficult to complete, and small mistakes may cause extra costs and repeated submissions. Citizens need clearer guidance and better support when preparing paperwork.

The Proposed Solution

ESBC-AI is a mobile-first assistive application that works alongside official services. It is designed to provide understandable guidance, support form preparation, validate documents, and help users follow their application progress.

Know

Understand procedures through plain-language Bangla and English answers grounded in official sources.

Prepare

Get guided form-filling support, document checks, and help identifying possible errors before submission.

Track and Pay

Explore application tracking and simulated fee-payment workflows.

Memory

Explore an opt-in document vault concept for reusing saved information securely.

AI Approach

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Retrieve relevant source material to ground answers in supporting information.

Document Intelligence

Explore scan-quality checks, OCR, structured extraction, and field-level confidence.

Agent Workflow

Use a tool-enabled workflow with checkpoints and human confirmation for important actions.

Evaluation

Evaluate response quality on a labelled Bangla and English question set.

Proposed Technology Stack

React, FastAPI, Pydantic, LangGraph, LangChain, Gemini, Chroma, OpenCV, Tesseract, and Docker.
